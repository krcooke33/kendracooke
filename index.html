I was young when the internet landed. It was clunky, wild, and full of promise.

Here we are again. In a moment that is clunky, wild, full of promise—and completely unhinged.

I'm an illustrator turned designer turned researcher turned product leader turned consultant turned AI strategist. Together, those masteries matter more now than ever.

I've been an illustrator, a designer, a researcher, a consultant, and now an AI strategist. Each one taught me something the next one needed. The curiosity came along for all of it.

Illustrator, then designer, researcher, consultant, and now AI strategist. I didn't leave any of them behind. Each is still a way I look at a problem.

Currently, I'm a Partner at SYPartners, where I co-lead our work on AI and lead our UX and Research. This site is where I keep some of my writing, research and explorations.

At a glance, it may seem like six careers, but I see it as my way of following the signals in moments of change and disruption. There is a thread that ties them all together, and that is curiosity.

  • Illustrator

    Practicing deep observation, building the habit of looking long enough to truly see

  • Designer

    Every picture is a set of decisions, building the taste to make the best ones

  • Researcher

    Listening and observing with intent, learning how to step out of my own shoes to understand others

curiosity

  • Product leader

    The art of breaking things apart to remake them into something useful to people

  • Consultant

    Having empathy for leaders and teams, deep enough to help them through tough or exceedingly aspirational times

  • AI strategist and maker

    Helping people meet the empty box with imagination, and build a relationship with technology that makes them more capable and more human
